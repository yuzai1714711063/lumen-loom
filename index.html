Our Story

One knot,
one breath,
one rug
at a time.

Lumen & Loom began in a small studio in Jaipur in 2019 with one quiet belief — that a rug should feel like sunlight on wood floors.

Today we work with 184 master artisans across Rajasthan, Kathmandu and the Atlas Mountains. We pay above fair-trade wage, in cash, every Friday. Each rug takes between 6 and 14 weeks to knot by hand. None are identical. We think that's the point.
